## Meads & Potions (separate system — buffs/burst effects, not food slots)
- Healing: Minor Healing Mead, Medium Healing Mead
- Stamina: Minor Stamina Mead, Medium Stamina Mead
- Resistance: Fire Resistance Barley Wine, Frost Resistance Mead, Poison Resistance Mead
- Tasty Mead (misc buff)
- Each has a "Mead Base" precursor item crafted first, then finished into the final mead

## Cooking stations (progression gate for what you can cook)
- Basic Cooking Station (fire + spit) — Meadows, wood only
- Cauldron — needs Forge nearby, unlocks soups/stews
- Iron Cooking Station — Swamp-tier (iron + chains)
- Spice Rack, Cauldron upgrades — later biome materials, unlock higher-tier recipes
- Food Preparation Table — Ashlands-tier station for endgame recipes

## Notes for Cooking skill design
- Stats here are what the FOOD gives when eaten, not cooking mechanics —
  still need to decide what the Cooking *skill* actually does (faster
  cooking? better stats on output? reduced burn/fail chance? bonus yield?)
- Should follow the same progression principles as the rest of the mod:
  soft-gate access (anyone can attempt any recipe they have materials
  for), gate the *rewards* (skill-based bonus effects, unique recipes,
  milestone unlocks) by Cooking level
